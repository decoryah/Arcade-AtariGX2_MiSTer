# Status

Against MAME 0.289 (the oracle), in simulation and in the Quartus tool flow; on the owner's MiSTer 20261030 and 20261031b
(both games play; the fixes the tests led to are below). 20261101 adds one fix checked on the bench (Frame pacing).

| JSA command NMI | the first release lost a command sent within one 6502 cycle of the 6502 reading the last (MAME's 6502 takes an NMI on every assert after a clear; T65 sees an edge only between two of its enables): the command stayed unread, every later command waited behind it and the game reset the sound board. `gx2_jsa.sv` now holds NMI high for two of T65's samples after each read of 2802 and after reset. The JSA alone replaying MAME's 45 s log of Moto Frenzy (coins, start, the race): no command lost (the first release stalled at 14.1 s); the sound's envelope against MAME's -wavwrite correlates 0.94 / 0.90 (left / right), the level a steady 1.42-1.46x MAME's to 15 s | `sim/run_jsa.sh` (reports the first lost NMI with the 6502's last cycles), `tools/cmp_audio.py` |
| Moto Frenzy's sound watchdog | the game's main loop (6DC8) sends 07 every ~35 frames and resets the sound board if that fails 5 times; the send fails while the reply to the heartbeat 03 (sent at the start of the VBLANK interrupt) is outstanding. MAME: the attempt 78 us after the heartbeat, the reply at ~89 us, so even MAME resets the board in the attract and the race select (frames 222, 884, 1257, 1629; silent from 14.8 to 27.6 s in its -wavwrite); in the race the attempt is at ~558 us. The core unpaced: the attempt at 47 us, every one failing (a reset every 372 frames, the music cutting out on hardware). CPU Speed Paced (2 / 16 steps a clock): the attempt at 134-142 us, the reply at ~92, 07 accepted at once (frame 224) and then every 64 frames to frame 1700, through the race select into the race, with no reset after the boot test's. Space Lords sends its 07 2.5-8.5 ms after the heartbeat and never resets in MAME, so Auto leaves it unpaced | `tools/lua/gx2_wdtime.lua`, `gx2_wdstate.lua` (MAME), `sim/run.sh ... +wdt +rate=n` |
| Frame pacing | MAME, Moto Frenzy's attract race (frames 1600-2000): the VBLANK interrupt starts an RLE draw every second frame, always at line 240, the frame bit alternating, and rewrites the per-line scroll entries in VBLANK (lines 242-254); the main loop's pass (object RAM and alpha writes) runs in the frame between: a steady 30 Hz, nothing changed on screen mid-frame. The bench (CPU paced 2 / 16, as Auto) the same through the race to frame 1800: a draw every second frame at line 240, never a gap of 3, the scroll entries in VBLANK, each draw done in at most 193 lines (37 % of the 524 it has; Space Lords' at most 121, its attract also a steady 30 Hz in both), and the pass in MAME's phase: its object writes 63-179 lines after /MOGO (MAME 45-133, the core unpaced 26-75), so the controls reach the screen as soon as in MAME (MAME's frame_number steps at line 0, the bench's frames at line 240: read unaligned they look a frame apart). The draw and the pass overlap: MAME renders every object at once on /MOGO, rle_objects took up to ~190 lines reading each object's fields as it reached it, so a field the pass rewrote before the draw got there showed a frame early: in the attract race 1-5 of the 67 objects in 9 of 29 draws, each time a pass wrote 345 words instead of 336 (every 8 frames; most likely the crowd's animation). rle_objects now copies the object RAM as the draw begins (6144 clocks, ~1.1 lines; neither game writes it within 20 lines of /MOGO) and draws from the copy: in the same race no field differs from the RAM at /MOGO, and the crowd at bench frame 1640 is pixel-identical to MAME's 1646 | `sim/run.sh ... +rate=2 +vlog=1500` (its `vid <f> stale` lines), `tools/lua/gx2_vlog.lua` |

## Open

- The hardware test (`dist/AtariGX2_MiSTer_20261101.zip`). Moto Frenzy's driving "sometimes jumpy and choppy" (owner,
  20261031b, CRT and RetroTINK 4K alike) is not reproduced on the bench: its race matches MAME's pacing and phase, and the
  one difference found (Frame pacing, above, now fixed) touched 1-5 objects every 8 frames, most likely the crowd.
- The sound's balance in Moto Frenzy's race: from 27.6 s the YM2151's engine (the sound CPU rewrites only its KC / KF and
  TL there) is ~8 dB quieter against the samples than in MAME; elsewhere the balance is MAME's, the level 3 dB higher.
- Unpaced (Space Lords, CPU Speed Full) the 68EC020 runs up to ~2.5x MAME's speed where the code is CPU-bound; the games pace
  on VBLANK, so only mid-frame timing (the per-line scroll table, random numbers) differs. Paced, Moto Frenzy's boot test
  runs ~2x as long as MAME's (the sound reset at frame 27, MAME 18).
